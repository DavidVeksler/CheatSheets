Reading the loom

  • Woven light is a visual metaphor, not physical strings, particle surfaces, or string theory.
  • Short fading trails show animation history, not measured trajectories.
  • Thin, steady connectors show families, never forces.
  • Interaction overlays use amber continuous, magenta braided, or cyan segmented accents.
  • Shape and color identify entries. Size, motion, and brightness do not measure physical properties. Colors do not encode QCD color charge.

Fermions and bosons: quantum-state sharing

Fermions obey exclusion: identical fermions cannot occupy the same complete quantum state (including spin). Identical bosons can share a state. This is not an ordinary repulsive force, and drawn positions are not complete quantum states.

The Higgs: field and particle

The Higgs field is distinct from its localized excitation, the Higgs boson. The Higgs mechanism contributes to the masses of elementary particles that couple to the field; it does not account for all ordinary matter's mass. Neutrino mass is outside this simplified demonstration.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Leptons and quarks are families inside fermions, not peers of fermions and bosons.
  • Not every lepton is electrically charged: neutrinos have zero electric charge.
  • Weak interaction changes particle flavors in allowed processes; an interaction does not connect every particle indiscriminately.
  • The Higgs is a boson, but not an ordinary force messenger.
  • Spin is intrinsic quantum angular momentum, not a tiny ball physically rotating.
  • Trails and family connectors are illustrations, not measured particle paths or decay routes.
