# Tesla product photography

Official Tesla product photos, downloaded by David Veksler from tesla.com (homepage cards and
mega-menu thumbnails) on 2026-09-22 and re-encoded here as WebP for page performance. These are
Tesla's own marketing photography, not original photography by this site; used editorially to
illustrate the corresponding spec section, credited to Tesla.

Source files (pre-rename, pre-encode) are kept in the local Downloads folder, not in this repo.
