Data Broker Opt-Out List

A comprehensive US checklist for removing your personal information from people-search websites and data aggregators.

General Instructions & Setup

CRITICAL: Do NOT use your primary email!
Use a disposable/alias email. Create one via Firefox Relay, SimpleLogin, or DuckDuckGo Email Protection before starting.
  • For each site below, navigate to the opt-out URL provided.
  • Search for listings matching [Your Full Name] in [Your City, State].
  • If multiple listings appear (e.g., current address, old addresses in other states), opt out of ALL of them.
  • When email verification is required, check the disposable inbox and click the verification link immediately — some expire within 15 minutes.
  • If a site requires a CAPTCHA that cannot be completed, flag it and move on.
  • Do not create accounts, enter payment information, or start free trials on any site.

Core Opt-Out Tasks

  1. Go directly to suppression.peopleconnect.us (PeopleConnect Suppression Center).
  2. Enter the disposable email address.
  3. Check email — click the verification link within 15 minutes.
  4. Enter your full legal name.
  5. Enter date of birth (required field — enter it if known, otherwise leave for manual follow-up).
  6. Select ALL matching records across all states you've lived in.
  7. Set each to "Suppressed" and confirm submission.

  1. Go to spokeo.com and search for your name.
  2. Find your profile listing. Copy the full profile URL (format: spokeo.com/[Name]/[State]/[City]/XXXXX).
  3. Go to spokeo.com/optout.
  4. Paste the profile URL into the form.
  5. Enter the disposable email address and complete the CAPTCHA.
  6. Submit, then check email and click the verification link.
  7. Note: If the profile is behind a paywall and you cannot get the URL, note this for later.

  1. Go to whitepages.com/suppression-requests.
  2. Search for your name and location.
  3. Paste the profile URL into the suppression form.
  4. Phone Verification: It will display a code on screen and call a phone number to verify. You must answer the call and enter the code.

  1. Go to Whitepages Support.
  2. Submit a support request asking to remove the Whitepages Premium listing for your name and address.

Professional Aggregators

Sites like LexisNexis supply data to law firms, investigators, and law enforcement. They have stricter opt-out processes.

LexisNexis / Accurint opt-out

Go to consumer.risk.lexisnexis.com/opt to request suppression under FCRA/state law. Requires identity verification (e.g., driver's license). Takes 3-5 weeks.

Thomson Reuters CLEAR opt-out

Opt-out via their Public Records Privacy Statement, which links to a Public Records Data Subject Portal. Similar identity verification process required.

Cotality (formerly CoreLogic) opt-out

Major property records aggregator. Opt-out via the Cotality privacy policy (cotality.com/legal/privacy-policy) under your state's privacy rights.

State Privacy Laws Leverage: As of August 2026, 24 states have enacted comprehensive privacy laws (AL, CA, CO, CT, DE, FL, IA, IN, KY, LA, MD, MN, MT, NE, NH, NJ, OK, OR, RI, TN, TX, UT, VA, and VT) — not all are yet in force, so check your state's effective date. Invoking your state law when submitting deletion requests creates a strict legal obligation to comply within ~45 days.
California DROP (Delete Request & Opt-out Platform): Live since January 2026 at privacy.ca.gov/drop/. California residents can submit a single request to delete personal data from 500+ registered data brokers simultaneously. Since August 1, 2026, brokers must access DROP and process deletion requests at least every 45 days or face fines.
CFPB Data Broker Rule — Withdrawn: The proposed federal FCRA rule that would have regulated data brokers as consumer reporting agencies was withdrawn May 15, 2025. No equivalent federal rule is in effect. State laws remain the primary legal leverage.

Post-Completion: Cache Removal

Even after removal from broker sites, your profile may still show up on search engines until they re-crawl the page.

  • Google: Go to Search Console Outdated Content Removal. Paste the old profile URLs you deleted.
  • Bing: Go to Bing Content Removal Tool and submit the same URLs.

Maintenance Schedule

Data brokers continuously refresh from public records (property sales, voter registration, DMV data). Re-run this entire checklist:

  • 30 days after initial completion
  • Every 90 days thereafter
  • Immediately after any major public record change (buying a house, registering to vote).
