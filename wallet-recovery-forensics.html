Wallet recovery forensics

Recovery is constraint engineering. First prove the wallet is not merely scanning the wrong path; then divide the remaining candidate space by a measured guess rate and accept the answer.

Write exact token/mask/position assumptions.

The wallet is often looking in the wrong place

Derivation path

The same seed creates different accounts under different purpose, coin, account, change, and index values.

Concrete use: Scan m/44'/0'/0', m/49'/0'/0', m/84'/0'/0', and m/86'/0'/0' only in an offline, systematic plan.

Failure mode: Random path guessing loses coverage and evidence.

Script type

Legacy, wrapped SegWit, native SegWit, and Taproot encode different output scripts and addresses.

Concrete use: Match a known historical receive address before scanning balances.

Failure mode: A valid seed can show zero when the wallet defaults to the wrong script family.

BIP39 passphrase

Every passphrase—including empty—derives a valid but unrelated wallet.

Concrete use: Test remembered spelling, normalization, spaces, case and language from a constrained list.

Failure mode: There is no checksum that tells you the passphrase is “close.”

Account/index range

Funds can sit beyond a wallet's default account or lookahead range.

Concrete use: Persist last-used indexes and scan beyond known activity only with a bounded range.

Failure mode: An unused gap can make later addresses invisible.

Wrong chain/coin type

Shared curve/address formats do not imply shared derivation or asset state.

Concrete use: Identify wallet software/version and the actual chain transaction history first.

Failure mode: Importing into a lookalike chain can expose keys without finding funds.

BIP39 search-space math

BIP39: entropy is 128–256 bits, words index a 2,048-word list, checksum length is ENT/32, and seed derivation uses PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA512 with 2,048 iterations. Checksum pruning is not password security.

Device, media & estate cases

Dead hardware, known seed

The seed/descriptor is the wallet; replace the device and verify derived address before moving.

Concrete use: Restore on a trusted spare, confirm a historical receive address, then migrate to fresh backup material.

Failure mode: Device brand does not bind funds if the interoperable backup is complete.

Unknown PIN, no seed

Retry counters and secure elements can turn experimentation into permanent wipe.

Concrete use: Stop, photograph model/firmware/condition, and obtain a specialist feasibility opinion before another attempt.

Failure mode: Online videos for another revision can destroy the only remaining state.

Damaged backup

BIP39 wordlist, prefix uniqueness, position and checksum provide reconstruction constraints.

Concrete use: Transcribe every plausible character/word from high-resolution images of the copy, not the original under repeated handling.

Failure mode: Cleaning chemicals and heat can erase remaining evidence.

Estate access

Legal authority and technical key access are independent requirements.

Concrete use: Keep a sealed locator/instruction record outside the will; use distributed key arrangements and annual restore drills.

Failure mode: Publishing a seed in a probate document destroys secrecy.

Recovery scam red flags

Unsolicited contact

Reply bots and impersonators target public loss reports.

Concrete use: Independently locate official contact channels; do not continue in direct messages.

Failure mode: Claimed law-enforcement affiliation is not verification.

Upfront unlock/tax fee

A fake platform displays a balance then demands payment to release it.

Concrete use: Do not pay; preserve messages, wallet addresses, domains and transaction evidence for reporting.

Failure mode: Additional payment compounds the loss and does not unlock a blockchain transfer.

Seed validator/sync site

The tool exfiltrates the only secret required to steal funds.

Concrete use: Use reviewed open-source tools offline on an isolated system and a copy.

Failure mode: A professional-looking domain and TLS do not make seed entry safe.

Guaranteed recovery

No one can recover a mathematically unconstrained private key.

Concrete use: Require a written feasibility model: candidate count, measured rate, time, cost and stop condition.

Failure mode: Confidence without constraints is sales fraud, not forensics.

Second-wave recovery

Victim lists are resold; a new actor claims to recover the first loss.

Concrete use: Assume anyone citing private details may have bought them; verify independently and report.

Failure mode: Prior victimization increases targeting, not legitimacy.

Safe recovery and prevention checklist

The annual restore drill is the only evidence that a backup, passphrase, derivation metadata, and procedure form a real recovery system.

  1. □ Stop using the only device/media; make verified read-only copies or a forensic image.
  2. □ Record wallet software, version, chain, script type, derivation path, known addresses, dates, and observed error.
  3. □ Scan path/script/account matrix before any cracking attempt.
  4. □ Compute candidate count and benchmark the exact KDF on named hardware before renting hardware.
  5. □ Run tools offline; never disclose seed, passphrase, private key, or unrestricted wallet file.
  6. □ Checkpoint long jobs and retain tested/excluded candidate sets.
  7. □ After recovery, move funds to a fresh wallet whose backup was independently verified.
  8. □ Store seed and passphrase in separate failure domains with durable, geographically redundant media.
  9. □ Record descriptor/path/software metadata alongside—not secret inside—the recovery instructions.
  10. □ Perform an annual restore to a spare device and verify a known first address.

Common mistakes & anti-patterns

Only-copy experimentation

A repair, parser or password attempt can corrupt the remaining evidence.

Concrete use: Image first; hash source and working copy.

Failure mode: Recovery cannot be repeated without provenance.

Cracking before scanning

The seed may be correct under another path, script, account or passphrase state.

Concrete use: Run the derivation matrix first.

Failure mode: Hardware spend cannot fix a wrong model.

Seed entered online

The attempt discloses spend authority.

Concrete use: Use reviewed tools offline and move recovered funds.

Failure mode: A checker can steal later, not immediately.

No stop condition

Search expands indefinitely as memories become guesses.

Concrete use: Precommit space, budget, benchmark and checkpoint.

Failure mode: Sunk cost is not new evidence.
